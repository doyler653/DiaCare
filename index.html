Overview

Welcome to DiaCare

DiaCare is a comprehensive diabetes management ecosystem designed to make daily care simpler, smarter and more connected. From glucose tracking and insulin logging to educational tools and caregiver access, DiaCare helps users take control of their health with confidence.

Core Features

Main Features

Glucose & Insulin Tracking

Log blood glucose readings and insulin doses quickly and clearly.

Smart Reminders

Stay on top of testing, medication, meals and daily diabetes routines.

Carbohydrate Counting

Support better food decisions with simple and accessible carb tracking tools.

Prescription Management

Keep medication details and schedules organised in one secure place.

Care Team Sharing

Enable permission-based data sharing with parents, carers and clinicians.

AI Insights & Trends

Identify patterns and highlight useful health insights over time.

Customisable Interface

Adapt the platform to suit personal preferences and user needs.

Ecosystem

How the DiaCare Ecosystem Works

DiaCare App

The main daily-use platform for tracking glucose, insulin, reminders, routines and personal diabetes management.

DiaFollow

A secure monitoring platform for parents, caregivers and clinicians to support shared care through permission-based access.

GlucoseShield

An educational game experience designed to help users build diabetes knowledge and management skills through interactive learning.

DiaWeb

An online resource hub providing educational content, community forums and support.
